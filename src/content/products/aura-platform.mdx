# AURA Digital Experience

AURA is a premium Digital Engineering Experience Platform designed to transcend the traditional developer portfolio.

## The Challenge

Traditional portfolios are static and fail to demonstrate engineering rigor, architectural thought processes, and product mindset. 

## The Solution

By building AURA, I created a dynamic, high-performance platform that acts as living proof of my engineering capabilities. It leverages Next.js, React Three Fiber, and Tailwind CSS to deliver a premium user experience.
